Exhibitors

OWASP
http://www.owaspla.org/

The Open Worldwide Application Security Project (OWASP) is the largest not-for-profit organization focused on improving the security of all software. Our mission is to improve software security through Open Source initiatives, community education, and networking. Everyone is welcome to participate in OWASP and all of our materials are available under a free and open software license. OWASP LA (Los Angeles) holds free monthly events on the 4th Wednesday of every month.

Percona
Silver Sponsor, Special Event Sponsor
http://www.percona.com

Percona builds open source database strategies and software to help you make sense of your database, on-premise or in the cloud, with unbiased solutions for MySQL, MariaDB, MongoDB, PostgreSQL and more. We help companies manage, deploy, and optimize databases to meet the ever-expanding needs of their customers.

Perforce
Gold Sponsor
http://www.perforce.com

Perforce helps enterprises secure and scale their Linux-based IT infrastructure. Our products, professional services, support, and OSS expertise empower millions of users to build, secure, fix, test, and improve at every key point in the tech lifecycle – so nothing stalls innovation.

Perl Mongers
https://www.facebook.com/groups/119158178096277

Perl is a general purpose high level programming language that has become an ubiquitous tool in the Linux world, being present by default in most, if not all, Linux distributions and other UNIX based operating systems.

Perl Mongers is a loosely based association of Perl User Groups all over the world. The Southern California Perl Mongers, from the cities of Los Angeles, Orange County, San Diego, and Thousand Oaks each do their part in spreading the use of Perl through regular technical and social meetings.

PostgreSQL
Silver Sponsor
http://www.postgresql.org

PostgreSQL is a powerful, open source object-relational database system. It has more than 20 years of active development and a proven architecture that has earned it a strong reputation for reliability, data integrity, and correctness. Its SQL implementation strongly conforms to the ANSI-SQL:2008 standard. It also has a host of extensions and advanced features. Best of all, PostgreSQL's source code is available under a liberal open source license: the PostgreSQL License.

Puppet by Perforce
Silver Sponsor
https://www.puppet.com/

Puppet by Perforce partners with organizations across the world to power desired state IT operations with secure infrastructure automation that enables them to thrive in today’s complex technology landscape.

Red Hat
Gold Sponsor, Travel Sponsor
http://www.redhat.com

Red Hat is the world’s leading provider of enterprise open source software solutions, using a community-powered approach to deliver reliable and high-performing Linux, hybrid cloud, container, and Kubernetes technologies. Red Hat helps customers integrate new and existing IT applications, develop cloud-native applications, standardize on our industry-leading operating system, and automate, secure, and manage complex environments.

Retia
Media Sponsor
https://retia.io/

Retia LLC has taught millions of beginners about ethical hacking while hosting the Null Byte, Hak5, & SecurityFWD YouTube channels. Our cat-themed Nugget platform is an open-source cybersecurity lab in your pocket made for learning hacking, electronics, and prototyping!

Rocky Linux
Silver Sponsor
https://rockylinux.org/

Rocky Linux is a community-driven Enterprise Linux distribution—stable enough for the largest enterprise to rely on it, and community-driven to ensure it stays accessible to all.

Sauce Labs
Reception Sponsor
http://www.saucelabs.com

Sauce Labs empowers you to develop, test, and deliver top-tier web and mobile apps at enterprise scale. Our unified platform simplifies automated testing and error monitoring, enabling you to streamline the entire development lifecycle. Gain deep insights, swiftly resolve issues, and dedicate more time to creating standout user experiences. With Sauce Labs, ensure quality is at the heart of your digital products, effortlessly.
