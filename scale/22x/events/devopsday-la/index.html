DevOpsDay LA 2025

Welcome to DevOpsDayLA, the heart of Southern California's DevOps movement!

DevOpsDayLA is back for its 14th year, bringing together the vibrant Southern California DevOps community. This annual event is Southern California’s premier conference dedicated to the DevOps community. It's a dynamic gathering where local practitioners, enthusiasts, and leaders come together to share insights, learn from one another, and explore the latest trends and practices in DevOps. As part of the global DevOpsDays series, DevOpsDayLA is more than just an event; it’s a movement that fosters collaboration, innovation, and continuous improvement across development and operations teams.

Event Details

  • Date: Friday, March 7, 2025
  • Time: 09:00 to 18:00
  • Location: Pasadena Convention Center

What is DevOpsDayLA?

DevOps is all about breaking down silos between development (Dev) and operations (Ops) teams, creating a more cohesive, efficient, and agile environment for software development and IT operations. At DevOpsDayLA, we emphasize the critical elements that make DevOps successful: automation, continuous integration/continuous delivery (CI/CD), a culture of shared responsibility, and the human factor that drives these processes forward.

Why Should You Attend?

  • Connect with the SoCal DevOps Community: DevOpsDayLA is a unique opportunity to network with fellow DevOps professionals, engineers, developers, operations experts, and tech leaders from Southern California and beyond. Whether you’re a seasoned professional or new to DevOps, this event offers a welcoming and inclusive environment where you can forge valuable connections, share your experiences, and learn from others.
  • Learn from Diverse, Cutting-Edge Content: We pride ourselves on offering a diverse lineup of presentations that cover a wide range of topics, from the latest advancements in DevOps practices to emerging technologies like AI integration and Platform Engineering. Our sessions are designed to be relevant, actionable, and forward-thinking, ensuring you walk away with new knowledge that you can immediately apply to your work.
  • Enhance Your Career: DevOpsDayLA isn’t just about technology—it’s also about your personal and professional growth. With dedicated talks on career upleveling, skills enhancement, and navigating the evolving tech landscape, you’ll gain insights that can help you advance in your career, take on new challenges, and achieve your professional goals.
  • Be Part of a Global Movement: As part of the global DevOpsDays series, DevOpsDayLA connects you to a larger network of DevOps professionals around the world. By attending, you’re not just participating in a local event—you’re joining a global conversation about the future of DevOps, contributing to the advancement of the field, and helping to shape the way organizations develop and operate software.
  • Support the Local Tech Community: DevOpsDayLA is a community-driven event that thrives on the contributions of its attendees, speakers, and sponsors. By attending, you’re supporting the growth and development of the local tech community, helping to foster innovation, and ensuring that Southern California remains a hub for cutting-edge technology and practices.

Who Should Attend?

DevOpsDayLA is designed for anyone involved in or interested in DevOps, including:

  • Developers and Software Engineers looking to learn more about operations and automation.
  • Operations and IT Professionals interested in improving efficiency and collaboration with development teams.
  • Tech Leaders and Managers seeking to understand the latest trends in DevOps and how to implement them in their organizations.
  • Students and Early-Career Professionals eager to gain new skills and insights in a rapidly evolving field.
  • DevOps Enthusiasts of all kinds who are passionate about technology and continuous improvement.

Stay Tuned for More Information

DevOpsDayLA is more than just a conference—it’s a celebration of the DevOps culture and community in Southern California. It’s a place where you can learn, grow, and connect with like-minded professionals who are as passionate about DevOps as you are. Whether you’re looking to stay ahead of the curve, advance your career, or simply be part of a dynamic community, DevOpsDayLA is the event for you.

Join us at DevOpsDayLA!! See our call for presenters, and be a part of the future of DevOps in Southern California! We can’t wait to see you there.

Check back for updates on speakers, session topics, and how to get involved as a volunteer, sponsor, or attendee. For additional information check out the DevOpsDayLA Event Page at devopsdays.org.
