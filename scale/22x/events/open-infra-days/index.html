OpenInfra Days

OpenInfra Days North America will take place in conjunction with SCaLE and is being jointly organized by the OpenInfra communities from the United States, Mexico, and Canada. Sessions will cover open source projects used to build data center infrastructure including OpenStack, Kubernetes, Linux, Kata Containers, StarlingX, Ceph, and many more! We encourage involvement from open source users, contributors, and companies from these countries for a lively networking and community-building opportunity!

The OpenInfra community exceeds 110,000 individuals from over 180 countries. Learn more about the global community and how you can participate within your local community!

OpenInfra Days North America will take place on March 6 & 7, 2025.

Diversity Tickets

Thanks to the support of our Diversity Sponsor, OpenMetal, we are able to offer 15 free tickets to attendees from traditionally underrepresented groups in the technology and/or open source communities as well as those who have been affected by the recent California fires. OpenStack’s 15th Birthday!

The OpenInfra Days North America planning committee has decided to donate the $1,000 OpenInfra Foundation sponsorship to World Central Kitchen to support those affected by the recent wildfires in Los Angeles.

We’re still celebrating! Join us for a casual happy hour at Der Wolf on Thursday, March 6, at 6:30 PMr, sponsored by RDO. It’s a great chance to connect with the community during OpenInfra Days North America. Stay tuned for more details, and thank you for your support!
