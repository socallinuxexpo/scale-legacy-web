The Free Software Foundation (FSF) is a nonprofit with a worldwide mission to promote computer user freedom and to defend the rights of all free software users.

GitHub
Platinum Sponsor
http://www.github.com

As the home for all developers, GitHub is the complete developer platform to build, scale, and deliver secure software. Over 94 million people, including developers from Fortune 100 companies, use GitHub to build and collaborate on everything from open source projects to the next big tech startup. With 330 million repositories and counting, GitHub is the place to shape the future of software, together.

Booth Number: 301
Grafana Labs
Silver Sponsor
http://www.grafana.com

Grafana Labs provides an open observability stack built around Grafana, the leading open source technology for dashboards. Grafana Labs helps companies manage observability strategies with the fully managed Grafana Cloud offering or self-managed Grafana Enterprise Stack, both featuring extensive enterprise data source plugins, dashboard management, metrics, logs, traces, and more.

Booth Number: 421
Isovalent
Silver Sponsor
https://isovalent.com/

Isovalent is the company founded by the creators of eBPF & Cilium. We build open source software and enterprise solutions solving networking, security, and observability needs for modern cloud native infrastructure. Google (GKE, Anthos), Amazon (EKS-A), and Microsoft (AKS) have all adopted Cilium to provide networking and security for Kubernetes. Cilium is used by platform engineering teams such as Adobe, Bell Canada, ByteDance, Capital One, Datadog, IKEA, Schuberg Philis, and Sky. As of 2024, Isovalent is part of Cisco.

Booth Number: 121
ITRDC

ITDRC harnesses the collective power of the technology community along with our professional network of volunteers to support communities impacted by disasters.

Jenkins

Kwaai

League of Professional System Administrators (LOPSA)
https://lopsa.org/

The League of Professional System Administrators (LOPSA) is a nonprofit corporation with members throughout the world. Our mission is to advance the practice of system administration; to support, recognize, educate, and encourage its practitioners; and to serve the public through education and outreach on system administration issues.

Booth Number: 128
Linbit
Silver Sponsor
http://www.linbit.com

LINBIT provides software-defined storage solutions for various platforms, such as Kubernetes & Proxmox, enabling scalable persistent storage across enterprise and cloud environments. LINSTOR helps organizations manage distributed block storage in complex containerized deployments. LINBIT’s solutions ensure high availability and performance for critical infrastructure. With our flagship product DRBD in the mainline Linux Kernel, LINBIT is rooted in the open source community.

Booth Number: 319
Linux Foundation Education
https://training.linuxfoundation.org/

Linux Foundation Education offers 20+ verifiable certifications that set the standards for the open source industry. Developed in cooperation with subject matter experts from industry and academia, our vendor-neutral exams champion command line proficiency. And, our robust, ever-evolving catalog of e-learning and instructor-led courses help industry professionals learn the skills, practical knowledge and critical thinking needed for success.
