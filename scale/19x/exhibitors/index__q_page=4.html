Nextcloud
https://github.com/nextcloud

Nextcloud is a suite of client-server software for creating and using file hosting services. It is enterprise-ready with comprehensive support options. Being free and open-source software, anyone is allowed to install and operate it on their own private server devices.

Observe
Gold Sponsor
https://www.observeinc.com

Observe discerns why applications and infrastructure are running the way they are from the data they emit. We enable engineers to spend more time coding features and less time investigating incidents. We reduce outages and issues with customer experience. And we leverage an elastic cloud architecture so that you only pay for what you use.

Octopus Deploy
Silver Sponsor
http://www.octopus.com

Octopus Deploy simplifies the hardest parts of continuous delivery and operations processes. Deploying software can be an error-prone and unreliable process, and Octopus delivers a vendor-agnostic solution that simplifies deployments and runbooks and makes release management easy. Octopus Deploy gives you confidence and control over your entire continuous delivery pipeline.

Open Source Initiative
https://opensource.org/

As a global non-profit, The Open Source Initiative (OSI) protects and promotes open source software, development and communities, championing software freedom in society through education, collaboration, and infrastructure, stewarding the Open Source Definition (OSD), and preventing abuse of the ideals and ethos inherent to the open source movement.

OpenNMS

OpenShot Video Editor
https://www.openshot.org/

OpenShot is an award-winning free and open-source video editor for Linux. Create videos with exciting effects, titles, audio tracks, and animations. Use our simple desktop editor to cut, slice, and combine videos, or automate your entire video production pipeline using our REST Cloud API.

If you love video editing, and are interested in getting involved in our project, please stop by and meet the team (and meet the creator and lead developer, Jonathan Thomas). We are always looking for volunteers, contributors, and those passionate about Linux video editing!
