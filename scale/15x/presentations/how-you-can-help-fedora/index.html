How You Can Help with Fedora
Perry Rivera, Alex Acosta
Audience:
Everyone
Topic:
General

This general workshop looks at some of the ways you can help shape the future of the Fedora 25 Linux distribution. 

We'll cover:

  • Ambassadors
  • Marketing
  • Development

Perry Rivera is a STEM Teacher for the Sylvan Learning Education company and is involved with the Fedora Ambassadors, San Fernando Valley Linux Users Group, and LETHAL (L.A. Ethical Technical Hackers And Leets).

Alex Acosta contributes to the Fedora Project as an ambassador spreading the voice of the project in his community. He also participates distributing Fedora media to remote locations without broadband access.
