Exhibitors

Temporal
Silver Sponsor
http://www.temporal.io

Temporal is a durable execution system that enables reliable and correct execution of software services and applications, allowing teams to manage application state at scale. Platform and application teams at companies like Netflix, Stripe, and Coinbase use Temporal to run application functions as workflows that inherit scalability, durability, and fault-tolerance.

Tetrate
Silver Sponsor
http://www.tetrate.io

Started by Istio and Envoy co-founders and maintainers to reimagine application networking, Tetrate is an enterprise service mesh company managing the complexity of modern hybrid cloud application infrastructure. Its flagship product, Tetrate Service Bridge, provides an edge-to-workload application connectivity platform to deliver business continuity, agility, and security for enterprises on the journey from traditional monoliths to the cloud. Customers get consistent, baked-in observability, runtime security and traffic management in any environment.

TinyGo
https://tinygo.org/

TinyGo is a project to bring the Go programming language to microcontrollers and modern web browsers by creating a new compiler based on LLVM.

TuxCare
Silver Sponsor
https://tuxcare.com/

TuxCare provides enterprise-grade Linux security solutions, helping organizations minimize downtime, stay compliant, and shrink their vulnerability exposure. With TuxCare's live patching solution, KernelCare Enterprise, teams can apply the latest security patches for all popular Linux distributions without having to reboot systems or schedule downtime, eliminating the need patching-related maintenance windows.

TuxDigital
Media Sponsor
http://www.tuxdigital.com

TuxDigital is a Educational media network that advocates for Open Source technology, Linux-based operating systems and more through teaching, training, and distribution of information. We produce a wide variety of content such as: technology related podcasts, one-off videos on our YouTube channels, and written content on FrontPageLinux.com like news, tutorials, reviews, opinion pieces & much more!

Ubuntu
Silver Sponsor
http://www.ubuntu.com

Ubuntu is a free operating system based on Debian, a popular Linux distribution. Its name comes from the Zulu word "ubuntu", loosely translated as "humanity", describing the ubuntu philosophy: "I am who I am because of those around me," a positive aspect of community.
