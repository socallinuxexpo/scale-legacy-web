Exhibitors

iXsystems
Silver Sponsor
https://www.ixsystems.com/

iX is an Open Source pioneer and the company behind TrueNAS, the world's most deployed storage software. Used by millions, TrueNAS lays the foundation for the Open Storage Era so that all organizations can access the benefits of true Data Freedom. TrueNAS enables users to harness the power of the legendary ZFS file system and provides unified and hyperconverged storage for private and cloud datacenters, with the reliability and performance demanded by virtualization, backup, and other data-intensive workloads.

Jenkins
http://www.jenkins.io

The leading open source automation server, Jenkins provides hundreds of plugins to support building, deploying and automating any project.

KDE
http://www.kde.org/

KDE, the KDE Software Compilation, is a contemporary desktop environment for UNIX, Linux and BSD workstations. KDE seeks to fulfill the need for an easy to use desktop for, similar to those found on Macintosh and Microsoft Windows operating systems

Kloudfuse
Silver Sponsor
https://www.kloudfuse.com/

Kloudfuse platform is the industry's first Unified Observability data lake for all your high cardinality observability streams.

KubeCost
Silver Sponsor
https://www.kubecost.com
League of Professional Systems Administrators
http://www.lopsa.org

The League of Professional System Administrators (LOPSA) is a nonprofit corporation with members throughout the world. Our mission is to advance the practice of system administration; to support, recognize, educate, and encourage its practitioners; and to serve the public through education and outreach on system administration issues.

LINBIT
Gold Sponsor
http://www.linbit.com

LINBIT’s® mission is to keep the digital world running. LINBIT accelerates businesses’ high availability and software-defined storage needs by transforming common standard hardware into lightning-fast, reliable block storage.

Linux Professional Institute
http://www.lpi.org

The Linux Professional Institute is a non-profit organization offering certifications for Linux, BSD and open-source software-based technologies.

LinuxChix LA
http://linuxchixla.org

Be Polite, Be Helpful. LinuxChixLA is the Los Angeles chapter under the international LinuxChix umbrella. LinuxChix focuses on supporting women in technology, specifically in the Open Source community. LinuxChix is a community that encourages participation by allowing the quieter voices a place to speak by holding fast to its core tenets, "be polite" and "be helpful.” LinuxChixLA conducts hands-on tutorials, installfests, and networking events through our Meetup page or on our website.

Loft Labs
Silver Sponsor
http://www.loft.sh

Loft Labs enables any organization to expand self-service access to Kubernetes from 10 to 10,000 engineers. Our team has created several open-source projects to support this mission, including DevSpace, vcluster, jsPolicy and kiosk. Our commercial product, Loft, ties these open-source projects together into a single Kubernetes platform that companies can use to provide engineers with secure but unimpeded access to cloud resources and to make the switch to truly cloud-native engineering practices.
