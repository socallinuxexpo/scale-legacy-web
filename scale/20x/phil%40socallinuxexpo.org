<div class="field field-name-body field-type-text-with-summary field-label-hidden"><div class="field-items"><div class="field-item even" property="content:encoded"><p>SCaLE 20x – the 20th Annual Southern California Linux Expo – will take place on <a href="https://www.visitpasadena.com/convention-center/"><strong>March 9-12, 2023 at the Pasadena Convention Center </strong></a>in Pasadena, CA</p>
<p>SCaLE is the largest community-run open-source and free software conference in North America. It is held annually in the greater Los Angeles area.</p>
<p><strong><strong><a href="https://forms.gle/3yLw8muwGTKC1qec6">Volunteer!</a></strong>  </strong>Interested in helping to plan and/or to volunteer for SCALE 20X?<strong> <a href="https://forms.gle/3yLw8muwGTKC1qec6">Get in touch</a> </strong>by either filling out this <a href="https://forms.gle/3yLw8muwGTKC1qec6">form</a> or sending us an <a href="mailto:staff@socallinuxexpo.org">email</a>.</p>
<p class="p1"><strong><a href="https://register.socallinuxexpo.org/reg6/">Attend</a>!</strong>  <a href="https://register.socallinuxexpo.org/reg6/">Register</a> to attend the conference.</p>
<p class="p1"><a href="cfp/index.html"><strong>Present!</strong></a> The SCALE 20x <a href="cfp/index.html">call for proposals</a> will close on December 2, 2022.</p>
<p class="p1"><a href="mailto:sponsorship@socallinuxexpo.org"><strong>Sponsor!</strong></a> Sponsorship and exhibitor opportunities are available for commercial and non-profit exhibitors. <a href="mailto:sponsorship@socallinuxexpo.org">Email us</a> for details.</p>

We've arranged a discounted rate at the <a href="https://www.hilton.com/en/attend-my-event/pasphhf-lnx5-1422b51a-d07c-4dc9-8883-a070eeac5624/">Hilton Pasadena</a>.</p>
