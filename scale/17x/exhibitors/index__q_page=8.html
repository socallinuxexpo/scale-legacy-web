Exhibitors

USENIX
USENIX

The USENIX Association has brought together engineers, system administrators, scientists, and technicians working on the cutting edge of the computing world since 1975. Our conferences are the essential meeting grounds for discussion of the most advanced research in all aspects of computing systems, and USENIX membership supports professionals and students alike in developing technical skills. We are committed to promoting open access to technical knowledge, while fostering diversity and the next generation of researchers.

Verizon Digital Media Services
Silver Sponsor
http://eng.vdms.io/

Playa Vista-based Verizon Digital Media Services delivers data for its customers around the world via the EdgeCast Content Delivery Network. The EdgeCast CDN provides a critical piece of Internet infrastructure with 130+ points of presence in 6 continents, for an aggregate egress bandwidth of over 67Tb/sec. In addition, VDMS’ Uplynk video streaming services offers end-to-end video streaming.

Vertical SysAdmin
http://www.verticalsysadmin.com

Vertical Sysadmin offers state-of-the-art IT consulting and training. We are GSA Certified and deliver custom courses at client locations around the globe. Our training covers a wide variety of technologies, including Git and GitLab, CI/CD Toolchains, Linux, AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform. Our engineering consulting effort focuses on DevOps and next-generation automation.

VictorOps
Gold Sponsor
http://www.victorops.com

With VictorOps, “on-call” isn’t a four-letter word. Our incident management software aligns log management, monitoring, chat tools, and more, for a single-pane-of-glass into system health. Using this IT and DevOps system data, we support automated alerting, centralized information, and essential documentation. Teams receive context-rich notifications and collaborate cross-functionally to empower fast, efficient incident resolution—and reduced downtime.

VideoLAN
VideoLAN

VideoLAN is a project that develops software for playing video and other media formats across a local area network (LAN). It originally developed two programs for media streaming, VideoLAN Client (VLC) and VideoLAN Server (VLS), but most of the features of VLS have been incorporated into VLC, with the result renamed VLC media player.

The project began as a student endeavor at École Centrale Paris (France), but after releasing the software under the free software/open source GNU General Public License, the project is now multinational with a development team spanning 20 nations.

VMWare
Gold Sponsor
http://www.vwware.com

VMware software powers the world’s complex digital infrastructure. The company’s compute, cloud, mobility, networking and security offerings provide a dynamic and efficient digital foundation to over 500,000 customers globally, aided by an ecosystem of 75,000 partners. Headquartered in Palo Alto, California, VMware celebrates twenty years of breakthrough innovation benefiting business and society. For more information please visit https://www.vmware.com/company.html.

WaveFront
Silver Sponsor
http://www.wavefront.com

Wavefront is metrics monitoring-as-a-service for cloud application environments. It gathers telemetry across the entire stack into a unified metrics store with the industry’s most advanced query analytics, rendering visualizations for anomaly detection, trend analysis, and intelligent alerting. Used by DevOps teams at SaaS leaders, it scales to thousands of users. Our customers include companies like Box, Workday, Intuit, Groupon, Lyft, DoorDash, and Postmates.

WhiteSource
Silver Sponsor
http://www.whitesourcesoftware.com/

WhiteSource integrates security into you DevOps pipeline to secure and manage open source components in your products. Our platform becomes part of your software development lifecycle (SDLC) and automates the entire process of open source components selection, approval, and management, including finding and fixing vulnerable components.

Yubico
Silver Sponsor
http://www.yubico.com

Yubico sets new global standards for simple and secure access to computers, servers, and internet accounts. The company’s core invention, the YubiKey, delivers strong hardware protection, with a simple touch, across any number of IT systems and online services. The YubiHSM, Yubico’s ultra-portable hardware security module, protects sensitive data stored in servers. Yubico is a leading contributor to the FIDO Universal 2nd Factor open authentication standard, and the company’s technology is deployed and loved by 9 of the top 10 internet brands and by millions of users in 160 countries.
